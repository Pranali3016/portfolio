about

From a classroom project to production agents.

I completed my B.E. in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at VPKBIET, Baramati, with a CGPA of 8.19. During college I built two projects that mattered to me: a crop-recommendation engine for farmers, and an Indian Sign Language interpreter that later got published at an IEEE conference and registered for copyright with the Government of India.

From there I moved through an ML internship at Oasis Infobyte, completed a full Python full-stack course, and joined Test Yantra as an Associate Software Engineer — where I built HireSense, a full-stack AI resume-intelligence system, along with a dealer-facing RAG chatbot and a job-search agent. Right now I'm deep in agentic AI: systems that don't just answer questions, but retrieve, decide, and take action.
